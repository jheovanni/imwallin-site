I'm wallin?

Privacy Policy

Effective September 16, 2026 · Dev0nti

The short version

I'm wallin? has no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics or advertising trackers. We do not collect or receive your personal information, and nothing you do in the app is sent to a server we run.

What stays on your device

Whether you have finished the welcome screens, and which tab and filter you last used, are stored on your device so the app behaves the way you left it.

Photos you take in Wallin cam are held on your device. A filtered copy is written to your device only when you tap Save or Share.

A clip you send with Text it is written to a temporary file on your device so the share sheet can attach it. iOS clears these temporary files.

If you add the home screen widget, Dev0nti photos are copied into a private container that only this app and its widget can read.

Deleting the app removes this data from your device.

Permissions we ask for

Camera: only so Wallin cam can take a photo. We do not record video or audio, and the camera is not used in the background.

Photos (add only): only so a filtered photo can be saved to your library when you tap Save. We do not read or browse your photo library.

You can change either permission at any time in iOS Settings. Declining them only disables the related feature.

When information goes to someone else

Sharing: when you tap Text it or Share, iOS opens the share sheet and you choose where the file goes. Anything you send is then handled by that app or service, such as Messages, under its own privacy policy. We do not see what you send or who you send it to.

Purchases: Wallin Plus is billed by Apple. Apple processes the payment and tells the app only whether your subscription is active. We never receive your payment details.

Store link: tapping Store opens the Dev0nti merch shop outside the app, where that shop's privacy policy applies.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will address it.

Your rights

Because we do not hold your personal information, there is usually nothing for us to look up, export, or delete. Deleting the app removes what is stored on your device. You may still contact us with any privacy request.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and the version in the app. Continuing to use the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Privacy questions: support@imwallin.com
